    Independent historical sources

    NBER business-cycle chronology: 34 official US recession peak/trough pairs, 1857–2020, included locally as a separate source file.

    NBER Macrohistory Database: pre-WWI/interwar economic measurements. Individual historical series require verified units, transformations and coverage before being charted.

    Methodology

    Series have different start dates and frequencies. Comparisons use monthly sampling, with the last observation available within each month. Missing history is left blank, never extrapolated.

    Historical data are current revised values, not real-time vintages. The analysis does not establish what investors knew at the time or prove predictive power.

    NBER event chronology is independently sourced; the quantitative series currently come from FRED, which republishes data from multiple originating institutions. Pre-1940 indicator gaps are shown explicitly, not filled with invented proxies. The ICE high-yield spread may have restricted history. The longer-running Moody's Baa–Treasury spread is provided as a distinct credit-stress proxy, not a like-for-like replacement.
